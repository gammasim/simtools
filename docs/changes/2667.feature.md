Simulate flashers (`simtools-simulate-flasher --run_mode full_simulation`) and illuminators (`simtools-simulate-illuminator`) with `--ray_tracing_backend obdeect`: obdeect makes the calibration light and traces it to the camera entrance, and sim_telarray simulates the camera with its optics bypassed (`Bypass_Optics=1` for single-mirror, `Bypass_Optics=2` for dual-mirror telescopes). Backends that trace calibration light provide `camera_photons(LightSourceRequest)`; `--instrument_seed` and `--simulation_seed` seed both backends.
