Add the quantum-efficiency envelope to the obdeect simulation chain: ``obdeect_atmosphere:efficiency_envelope=qe`` thins photons by the telescope QE divided by a headroom of 1 / (1 + 6 qe_variation), and sim_telarray then reads the QE table divided by the envelope. The envelope is on by default for chains ending in ``sim_telarray:bypass_optics=3`` and off otherwise; ``efficiency_envelope=none`` or ``qe`` overrides the default.
