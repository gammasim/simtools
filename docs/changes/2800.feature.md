Run simulations as a chain of stages: ``--simulation_software`` takes the stages in order, each as ``STAGE`` or ``STAGE:KEY=VALUE,...`` (e.g. ``corsika obdeect_atmosphere obdeect_optics sim_telarray:bypass_optics=3``); converters between photon formats are inserted automatically. New stages trace photons with obdeect through the atmosphere and the telescope optics and generate flasher and illuminator light, so that sim_telarray simulates only the camera and electronics. ``simtools-simulate-flasher`` (full simulation) and ``simtools-simulate-illuminator`` accept these chains. Chains that apply an efficiency factor twice are rejected, and the installed sim_telarray is checked for camera-only support (``Bypass_Optics=3``) before jobs are written. Optional quantum-efficiency envelope (``obdeect_atmosphere:efficiency_envelope=qe``).
